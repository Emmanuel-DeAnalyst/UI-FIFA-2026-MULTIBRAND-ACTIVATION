# 📊 UI FIFA Multibrand In-Store Activation Dashboard

**Project Category:** Business Intelligence | Sales Analytics | Marketing Performance Measurement

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to monitor and evaluate the performance of the UI FIFA Multibrand In-Store Activation across four locations: Lagos Mainland, Lagos Island, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.

The dashboard consolidates consumer reach, sales volume, revenue achievement, brand-level performance, and weekly sales trends into a centralised reporting solution. It enables stakeholders to compare actual performance against targets, identify high-performing locations and brands, monitor weekly sales progress, and evaluate overall campaign effectiveness.

## 📈 Key Performance Highlights

- **Consumer Reach:** 169,188 consumers reached against a target of 165,704, achieving 102% of the target.
- **Sales Volume:** 232,479 units sold against a target of 199,440, achieving 117% of the target.
- **Sales Value:** ₦986.83M generated against a target of ₦801.60M, achieving 123% of the target.
- **Top-Performing Location:** Lagos Mainland led with approximately 96,000 units sold and ₦391M in sales value.
- **Brand Performance:** Dove contributed approximately ₦389.27M, followed by Rexona at ₦340.97M and Axe at ₦256.59M.
- **Weekly Sales Trends:** Monitored sales volume against weekly targets throughout the activation period.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Consumer Reach Analysis
- Achieved 102% of the overall consumer reach target.
- Recorded 169,188 consumers reached against a target of 165,704.
- Evaluated campaign reach to assess the effectiveness of in-store activation activities.

### 2. Sales Volume Analysis
- Achieved 117% of the overall sales volume target.
- Recorded 232,479 units against a target of 199,440.
- Lagos Mainland recorded the highest sales volume, followed by Lagos Island, Abuja, and Port Harcourt.

### 3. Sales Value Analysis
- Generated ₦986.83M against a target of ₦801.60M.
- Achieved 123% of the sales value target.
- Lagos Mainland contributed the highest sales value at approximately ₦391M.

### 4. Brand Performance Analysis
- **Dove:** ₦389.27M
- **Rexona:** ₦340.97M
- **Axe:** ₦256.59M

Dove recorded the highest sales value, followed by Rexona and Axe, providing visibility into the relative commercial contributions of the three brands.

### 5. Weekly Sales Trend Analysis
- Compared actual weekly sales volume against established targets.
- Identified fluctuations in weekly performance across the activation period.
- Monitored sales consistency and highlighted periods of stronger or weaker performance.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- KPI monitoring for consumer reach, sales volume, and sales value.
- Actual-versus-target performance analysis.
- City-level sales volume and revenue comparisons.
- Brand-level sales value analysis.
- Brand sales volume distribution.
- Weekly sales trend monitoring.
- Visual performance tracking to support campaign evaluation.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of campaign performance, enabling stakeholders to assess target achievement, compare geographical results, evaluate brand contributions, and monitor weekly execution.

These insights support informed decisions on resource allocation, field team performance, sales optimisation, and future in-store activation planning.
